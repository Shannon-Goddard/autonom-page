How-To: Google Search & Analytics

Get your site into Google search results and see who's visiting. Both are free. Neither requires any coding.

Two separate things

This guide covers two different Google tools that people often confuse:

  • Google Search Console — tells Google your site exists and lets you see what search terms people use to find you. Free. No code on your site.
  • Google Analytics — shows you how many people visit your site, where they came from, and what they looked at. Free. Adds a small tracking script to your pages.

You don't need both. Search Console is more useful if you care about being found on Google. Analytics is more useful if you want to understand your visitors. Most people set up both.

Both are optional. Your Autonom site already has JSON-LD structured data built in, which helps Google understand your content even without Search Console. These tools just give you more visibility and control.

Part 1 — Google Search Console

Tells Google your site exists. Shows you what people search to find you.

Step 1 — Add your site

  1. Go to search.google.com/search-console and sign in with your Google account.
  2. Click Add property.
  3. Choose URL prefix and enter your site URL — either https://yourusername.github.io or your custom domain like https://yourname.com.
  4. Click Continue.

Step 2 — Verify ownership

Google needs to confirm you own the site before it shows you data. The easiest method for Autonom sites is the HTML tag method:

  1. In the verification screen, click HTML tag to expand it.
  2. You'll see a meta tag that looks like this:
    <meta name="google-site-verification" content="abc123xyz..." />
  3. Copy just the content value — the part between the quotes after content=. It's a long string of letters and numbers.
  4. Log in to your Autonom site, open the Settings panel, and paste it into the Google Search Console verification code field.
  5. Hit save. Your site will update within a minute.
  6. Go back to Search Console and click Verify.

If verification fails, wait 2–3 minutes after saving in Autonom and try again. The site needs to rebuild on GitHub Pages before Google can read the tag.

Step 3 — Submit your sitemap

A sitemap tells Google exactly which pages your site has. Your Autonom site generates one automatically.

  1. In Search Console, click Sitemaps in the left sidebar.
  2. In the Add a new sitemap field, type sitemap.xml.
  3. Click Submit.

Google will crawl your sitemap and start indexing your pages. This can take anywhere from a few hours to a few days for a new site. Once it's indexed, your site will appear in Google search results.

Part 2 — Google Analytics

See how many people visit, where they came from, and what they looked at.

Step 1 — Create a Google Analytics account

  1. Go to analytics.google.com and sign in with your Google account.
  2. Click Start measuring.
  3. Enter an account name — this is just for you, use anything like "My Website".
  4. Click Next, then set up a Property. Give it a name (your site name), choose your timezone and currency.
  5. Click Next, fill in the business details (just pick whatever fits), then click Create.
  6. Accept the terms.

Step 2 — Get your Measurement ID

  1. After creating the property, Google will ask you to set up a data stream. Choose Web.
  2. Enter your site URL and a stream name, then click Create stream.
  3. You'll see a Measurement ID — it looks like G-XXXXXXXXXX.
  4. Copy that ID.

Step 3 — Add it to your Autonom site

  1. Log in to your Autonom site and open the Settings panel.
  2. Paste your Measurement ID into the Google Analytics ID field.
  3. Hit save.

That's it. Autonom adds the Analytics tracking script to every page of your site automatically. Give it 24–48 hours and you'll start seeing visitor data in your Analytics dashboard.

Analytics data only shows up for real visitors — not you browsing your own site. If you want to exclude your own visits, Google Analytics has a filter for that under Admin → Data Streams → your stream → Configure tag settings → Define internal traffic. That's optional and a bit more advanced.

What to expect

New sites take time to get traction in Google. Don't panic if you're not showing up in search results on day one — that's completely normal. Here's a realistic timeline:

  • Day 1–3: Google crawls your sitemap and starts indexing your pages.
  • Week 1–2: Your site starts appearing in search results for your name and exact phrases from your bio.
  • Month 1+: If you add content regularly (gallery updates, music, blog-style bio updates), Google starts ranking you for more terms.

The JSON-LD structured data that Autonom builds into your site gives you a head start — Google can understand exactly who you are, what you do, and what your site is about without having to guess from your content.
